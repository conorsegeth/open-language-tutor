# Assessment

## Review Evidence

Read the learner’s language record. Use the relevant read-only catalog: [Reading and Writing](../../../reference/reading-writing.md) or [Speaking and Listening](../../../reference/speaking-listening.md).

- Assess what the learner actually did. Asking for a translation or copying an answer does not show independent ability.
- Consider accuracy, consistency, and how much help the learner needed. Do not change a rating based on a single slip or a lucky answer.
- Assess only what the interaction shows. Typed answers do not demonstrate speaking or listening, and voice answers do not demonstrate reading or writing.

## Update Progression

Put each catalog ID in one bucket under **Progression** for the matching domain. Leave untested abilities out.

- **Strong:** The learner has demonstrated the skill confidently and consistently.
- **Moderate:** The learner demonstrated some competence in the skill but is not yet fully consistent.
- **Weak:** The learner has demonstrated difficulty with the skill.

Move an existing flag at most one bucket at a time, and only when new evidence supports the change.

## Update History

- **Domain History:** Note useful practice, how the learner did, and what to try next. A note can be worth saving even when no rating changes.
- **General History:** Keep notes that apply across domains.
- **Goals & Motivations:** Update this section when the learner shares a language-specific goal, interest, or motivation.

Keep notes brief. If nothing meaningful changed, leave the record alone.
