# Progression Flags (Reading & Writing)

This read-only catalog describes reading and writing abilities by proficiency level.

## Reading

### Beginner (A1)

- `read_familiar_words`: Can recognise familiar words and common written expressions in simple contexts.
- `read_basic_phrases`: Can understand very short, simple phrases when rereading is possible.
- `read_simple_messages`: Can understand the main point of a very short, simple personal message.
- `read_simple_questions`: Can understand basic written questions about familiar information.
- `read_simple_notices`: Can interpret very simple notices, labels, or signs using familiar language.
- `read_find_basic_details`: Can locate clearly presented basic facts such as a time, quantity, or place in a short text.
- `read_short_descriptions`: Can understand a very brief description made of familiar words and simple sentences.
- `read_single_step_instructions`: Can follow a short, straightforward written instruction in a familiar context.

### Elementary (A2)

- `read_high_frequency_texts`: Can understand short texts mainly composed of common language about familiar situations.
- `read_routine_messages`: Can understand the purpose and key details of a short routine written message.
- `read_simple_correspondence`: Can understand short personal correspondence about familiar experiences or arrangements.
- `read_simple_descriptions`: Can understand simple descriptions of people, places, objects, or everyday events.
- `read_short_narratives`: Can follow the sequence of events in a short, straightforward written story.
- `read_find_predictable_information`: Can locate specific predictable information in a short practical text.
- `read_simple_instructions`: Can follow a short sequence of clearly presented written instructions.
- `read_explicit_preferences`: Can recognise clearly stated preferences or opinions in a simple written text.
- `read_infer_familiar_word`: Can infer the likely meaning of an unfamiliar common word from a simple context.

### Intermediate (B1)

- `read_straightforward_factual_texts`: Can understand the main ideas of straightforward factual texts on familiar or personally relevant subjects.
- `read_factual_supporting_details`: Can identify important supporting details in a clearly organised factual text.
- `read_gather_across_sections`: Can combine straightforward information from different parts of one text.
- `read_personal_accounts`: Can understand accounts of experiences, feelings, and reactions in connected written language.
- `read_linear_narratives`: Can follow the plot and chronology of a clearly structured narrative.
- `read_stated_viewpoints`: Can identify a writer's explicitly stated opinion and supporting reasons.
- `read_simple_argument_structure`: Can recognise a clearly signalled conclusion and the main steps leading to it.
- `read_familiar_procedures`: Can follow straightforward written instructions for a process of a familiar type.
- `read_infer_meaning_context`: Can infer the meaning of an unfamiliar expression when surrounding context provides strong clues.

### Upper Intermediate (B2)

- `read_detailed_arguments`: Can follow a sustained written argument and understand its supporting evidence.
- `read_contrasting_positions`: Can distinguish and compare different positions within a written discussion.
- `read_fact_vs_persuasion`: Can recognise when a text is reporting information versus attempting to persuade.
- `read_discourse_structures`: Can recognise cause-effect, problem-solution, and concession structures in extended prose.
- `read_complex_instructions_familiar`: Can follow lengthy instructions with conditions and warnings in a familiar domain.
- `read_narrative_development`: Can understand detailed narrative development and relationships among events or characters.
- `read_register_and_tone`: Can recognise differences in tone and formality across a range of written texts.
- `read_nonliteral_language`: Can interpret common figurative or idiomatic language when contextualised.

### Advanced (C1)

- `read_lengthy_complex_texts`: Can understand lengthy, structurally complex texts, including on less familiar subjects, with rereading where necessary.
- `read_implicit_attitudes`: Can identify implied viewpoints, attitudes, and evaluations in complex writing.
- `read_complex_argumentation`: Can follow complex lines of reasoning, including qualifications and underlying assumptions.
- `read_specialised_texts`: Can understand demanding specialised material when reference tools are available for unfamiliar terms.
- `read_complex_instructions`: Can interpret lengthy instructions or procedures on unfamiliar matters, including exceptions and conditions.
- `read_subtle_style`: Can appreciate purposeful stylistic and register differences in complex texts.
- `read_dense_cross_references`: Can follow relationships between ideas across dense passages and cross-referenced sections.
- `read_literary_subtext`: Can interpret important implied meanings and themes in sophisticated narrative or creative writing.
- `read_complex_correspondence`: Can understand detailed formal or informal correspondence containing nuance and indirect meaning.

### Proficient (C2)

- `read_fine_implications`: Can distinguish very fine differences between explicit meanings and implied consequences.
- `read_complex_ambiguity`: Can recognise deliberate ambiguity, competing interpretations, and layered meanings in writing.
- `read_stylistic_nuance`: Can appreciate subtle rhetorical, stylistic, and genre-specific effects across varied writing.
- `read_allusions_irony`: Can interpret sophisticated allusions, irony, and figurative language where context permits.
- `read_intricate_argumentation`: Can critically follow intricate arguments and the interaction of multiple perspectives.
- `read_unfamiliar_varieties`: Can understand highly colloquial or less familiar written varieties with appropriate contextual adjustment.
- `read_dense_specialised_sources`: Can interpret the fine points of complex specialised writing beyond their main area of familiarity.

## Writing

### Beginner (A1)

- `write_basic_personal_details`: Can write a few simple statements giving basic information about themselves.
- `write_simple_phrases`: Can produce short, familiar phrases or simple independent sentences.
- `write_basic_questions`: Can write straightforward questions requesting basic information.
- `write_short_messages`: Can write a very short message communicating one simple point.
- `write_simple_descriptions`: Can write a few simple words or sentences describing a familiar person, object, or place.
- `write_basic_needs`: Can express a simple need, wish, or preference in writing.
- `write_basic_responses`: Can respond briefly in writing with agreement, disagreement, thanks, or acknowledgement.

### Elementary (A2)

- `write_link_simple_sentences`: Can connect simple sentences using basic sequencing, addition, or contrast.
- `write_routine_messages`: Can compose a short message that requests or communicates straightforward practical information.
- `write_simple_correspondence`: Can compose a short personal message or reply using routine conventions.
- `write_linked_descriptions`: Can describe familiar surroundings, people, or situations in several linked sentences.
- `write_routines`: Can describe habitual actions and their frequency in simple written language.
- `write_past_experiences`: Can write a short account of a past event or experience.
- `write_future_plans`: Can state a straightforward plan or intention in writing.
- `write_preferences_with_reasons`: Can express a preference and give a basic reason in writing.
- `write_simple_sequence`: Can describe the steps of a familiar process or sequence of events in basic written language.
- `write_basic_comparisons`: Can compare familiar options in short, simple sentences.

### Intermediate (B1)

- `write_connected_text`: Can produce a clear connected text on a familiar subject using a logical sequence of ideas.
- `write_detailed_familiar_description`: Can describe familiar experiences or situations with supporting details.
- `write_narrate_events`: Can narrate events in a coherent order using appropriate time relationships.
- `write_explain_feelings`: Can describe feelings or reactions and give straightforward explanations for them.
- `write_state_support_opinion`: Can state an opinion and support it with several understandable reasons.
- `write_simple_pros_cons`: Can explain basic advantages and disadvantages of an option in writing.
- `write_practical_correspondence`: Can write a clear request, explanation, or response about a practical matter.
- `write_report_factual_information`: Can summarise straightforward factual information in a short, organised text.
- `write_summarise_familiar_source`: Can convey the main points of a straightforward source in their own words.
- `write_revise_for_clarity`: Can revise a draft to clarify its main message and correct noticeable inconsistencies.

### Upper Intermediate (B2)

- `write_clear_detailed_text`: Can produce clear, detailed writing on a broad range of subjects.
- `write_structured_argument`: Can develop an argument with a clear position and appropriately organised support.
- `write_evaluate_alternatives`: Can analyse alternatives, including advantages, drawbacks, and trade-offs.
- `write_synthesise_sources`: Can combine information from several sources into a coherent written account.
- `write_complex_explanations`: Can explain an involved process or issue with relevant detail and qualifications.
- `write_cohesive_transitions`: Can link paragraphs and arguments with effective cohesive devices.
- `write_adapt_tone`: Can adjust tone and formality to suit common audiences and purposes.
- `write_detailed_correspondence`: Can exchange views and information in clear, appropriately structured correspondence.
- `write_nuanced_description`: Can express meaningful distinctions and relationships in detailed descriptive writing.
- `write_edit_consistency`: Can revise longer writing for clarity, organisation, consistency, and appropriate wording.

### Advanced (C1)

- `write_well_structured_complex`: Can produce well-structured extended writing on complex subjects with clear priorities.
- `write_develop_complex_case`: Can develop a qualified position with subsidiary arguments, evidence, and counterpoints.
- `write_integrate_multiple_perspectives`: Can compare and integrate multiple perspectives without obscuring their differences.
- `write_precise_register`: Can adjust register, style, and degree of formality precisely for an intended readership.
- `write_implicit_emphasis`: Can convey subtle attitudes and implications through careful wording and emphasis.
- `write_advanced_cohesion`: Can sustain clear coherence through sophisticated transitions and thematic development.
- `write_complex_synthesis`: Can combine complex information from different sources while keeping attribution and meaning clear.
- `write_effective_revision`: Can redraft complex writing to improve structure, precision, readability, and persuasive effect.

### Proficient (C2)

- `write_smooth_complex_prose`: Can produce complex text that flows naturally and remains easy to follow despite demanding content.
- `write_fine_shades_meaning`: Can express subtle distinctions and qualifications with consistently precise wording.
- `write_rhetorical_structure`: Can organise sophisticated writing for strong logical and rhetorical impact.
- `write_integrate_competing_views`: Can weave multiple competing perspectives into a coherent, nuanced written treatment.
- `write_stylistic_control`: Can vary style and register flexibly to achieve specific effects for different audiences.
- `write_figurative_ironic_effects`: Can use idiom, figurative language, humour, or irony appropriately to shape meaning.
- `write_controlled_ambiguity`: Can use or resolve ambiguity deliberately, according to communicative purpose.
- `write_critical_synthesis`: Can produce a nuanced critical synthesis of complex source material with clear attribution.
- `write_edit_fine_precision`: Can refine sophisticated writing for subtle precision, tone, and economy without losing meaning.
